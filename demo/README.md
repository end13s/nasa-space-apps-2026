# demo/ — Sofia + Julia

Submission rules that decide eligibility, not taste:

- **Either** a video of **30.0 seconds or less**, **or** up to **7 slides**
  (title and intro slides count). Over either limit = not eligible for Global
  Judging.
- Hosted **externally** (YouTube, Drive, GitHub) with a public link that needs
  **no login**. Test it in an incognito window, on a phone, before submitting.
- **English**, or English subtitles.
- **No NASA logos or branding** anywhere in generated content.
- **No images, video or likeness of anyone under 18.**
- Any AI-generated image or video needs a **visible watermark**.
- The project page accepts images up to 10 MB — not videos, not documents.

What lands in this folder: `script.md`, the raw screen recordings (J15), the
final cut, and the backup 7-slide deck (S12). Keep the recordings out of git if
they are large — Drive link in `#science` instead.
