# 30-second demo video — script and shot list

**Hard rules (eligibility, not taste):** ≤ 30.0 seconds. English subtitles burned
in. Hosted externally with a public link that needs no login. No NASA logo
anywhere. Nobody under 18 on screen. Any AI-generated visual carries a visible
watermark.

Voiceover is ~75 words — that is the ceiling for 30 seconds at a natural pace.
Record it once, flat and unhurried; a rushed read is the most common way these
go over time.

**Running total:** 73 words. Time it before you record; if it runs over, cut the
word "live" and the second half of the 0:19 line first.

## The shot that matters

**0:11–0:19 is the whole project.** A judge who sees only that one shot should
understand the claim. Make sure the three columns are legible at 1080p and that
**Cannot test is visually dominant** — if the eye lands on the green column
first, re-frame the shot.

Second most important: **0:19–0:25**. Hold on `ti_class` if it is visible, with
its perfect match score sitting in the Cannot test column. A variable that
matches exactly and still cannot be used is the clearest single image of what
this project does.

## Recording notes

- 1080p, 60 fps, cursor highlighting on.
- Record each shot separately and cut, rather than one continuous take — a
  mis-click costs one shot, not the whole run.
- Let the heatmap finish painting before moving the cursor; the pause reads as
  confidence, and it shows the speed honestly.
- If the Earth data is still synthetic at recording time, **do not hide it** —
  the About modal states it, and a judge who finds it themselves will trust
  nothing else on the page. Better to say "synthetic Earth grid, pipeline
  complete" in the project description than to be caught.

## Subtitles

Burn them in, do not rely on a player's caption track. Match the voiceover word
for word — judges may watch muted.

## Backup: the 7-slide deck

If the video cannot be finished, up to 7 slides is equally eligible. Same
narrative, one idea per slide:

1. Title + team
2. The problem — analog sites are chosen by tradition, not measurement
3. How it works — fingerprint, compare, map (one diagram)
4. The data — NASA, ESA, JAXA sources
5. **The proxy card** — Supports / Conditional / Cannot test (screenshot)
6. Validation — the numbers, honestly, including what failed
7. Limits and future work + team

Slide 5 is the one that carries the project. If only one slide can be good,
make it that one.
