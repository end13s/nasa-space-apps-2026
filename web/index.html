What this is

Pick a site on the Moon or Mars. Analog Atlas scores every land cell on Earth against it, for a stated experiment, and says what each Earth site supports, what is conditional on a stated correction, and what it cannot test at all.

It does not claim any Earth site reproduces another world. Gravity, vacuum and radiation are listed as untestable on every card.

Data

  • Moon: NASA LRO — LOLA topography and 1064 nm albedo, Diviner temperatures and thermal products, PGDA permanently-shadowed-region maps; USGS Unified Geologic Map.
  • Mars: NASA MGS MOLA and TES, Mars Odyssey THEMIS; MOLA–HRSC blended DEM (USGS); rover ground truth from Curiosity and Perseverance; USGS global geologic map.
  • Earth grid in use: NOAA ETOPO 2022 (60″ elevation; ice thickness from surface minus bedrock) and WorldClim 2.1 (10′ climate normals). Slopes are measured at a ~1.85 km baseline and temperatures are 2 m air temperature, not land-surface temperature.
  • Imagery: NASA Moon Trek, Mars Trek and GIBS.

Use of AI

The first project plan, and a later proposal to reframe the project around experimental proxies, were drafted with Anthropic's Claude; the team discussed both and chose the experiments, features and transfer rules. Claude (Claude Code) helped write and review parts of the engine, the data pipelines and this interface; CesiumJS agent skills helped with the globes. All of it was read, tested and corrected by the team. No AI-generated images are used.
